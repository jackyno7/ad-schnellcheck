High A4 · Privileged accounts whose password never expires (3 hits)

Passwords of administrator accounts that never expire often stay unchanged for years - even after staff changes.

Recommendation: Remove the option "Password never expires". For the built-in Administrator account, set a long random password and store it safely (break-glass account).

Recommendation: Replace these systems or disconnect them from the network. If the device no longer exists, delete the computer account.

High K2 · Password not required (1 hits)

These enabled accounts may have an empty password - the password policy does not apply to them. Disabled accounts (e.g. Exchange system accounts) are excluded.

Recommendation: Remove the option: Set-ADUser <account> -PasswordNotRequired $false, then set a new password.
